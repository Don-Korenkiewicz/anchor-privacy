Anchor Care: Today — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Anchor Care: Today ("Anchor", "the app") is a personal app built by an independent developer, not a company or medical organization. These terms are written in plain language because that's the whole point of the app.

What the app is for

Anchor is a simplified companion to MyChart, showing your own appointments and medications in a large-text, easy-to-use format. It is a convenience tool for viewing and reviewing information that already exists in your healthcare provider's system — it does not replace your patient portal, your provider, or professional medical advice.

Not a medical device, not medical advice

Anchor does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. It displays information your healthcare provider has already recorded. Always follow your provider's actual instructions, and contact your provider's office directly (not this app) for anything urgent or time-sensitive.

Your MyChart connection

You connect Anchor to your MyChart account through your healthcare provider's own secure login page. Anchor never sees or stores your MyChart username or password. You can disconnect at any time by deleting the app, which removes the stored access token from your device.

Refill requests

If Anchor offers a "Request Refill" feature, submitting a request through the app is the same as submitting one through any other channel — it is not guaranteed to be approved, and your provider's office makes the actual decision, on their own timeline.

No warranty

Anchor is provided "as is," built and maintained by one person as a personal project, without any warranty of accuracy, availability, or fitness for a particular purpose. While care is taken to display your information correctly, always verify anything important directly with your healthcare provider.
