Anchor Care: Today — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Anchor Care: Today ("the app", "Anchor") is a personal app built by an independent developer to make it easier for older adults, including people living with memory conditions, to see what's happening with their healthcare today — without the complexity of a full patient portal. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it's used, in plain language.

What the app accesses

  • Appointment and medication information — Read directly from your healthcare provider's Epic MyChart system, with your explicit permission, so the app can show you what's happening today.

Where your information is stored

Anchor does not store your health information on any server. Your appointment and medication details are fetched directly from your healthcare provider's Epic system each time the app is used, and live only on your own device while you're using the app. A secure login token (not your username or password) is stored in your device's Keychain, Apple's secure on-device storage, so you don't have to log in again after the first time.

What we don't do

  • We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software.
  • We don't sell or share your information with third parties.
  • We don't build advertising profiles.
  • We never see or store your MyChart username or password — login happens directly on your healthcare provider's own page.

Third-party services the app uses

  • Your healthcare provider's Epic/MyChart system — The app connects to your provider's Epic system using a secure, patient-authorized login (the same kind of connection used by other MyChart-connected apps). You log in directly on your provider's own real login page — Anchor never sees or stores your MyChart username or password, only a secure token that lets it check your information again without you having to log in every time.

Your control over your information

You can disconnect the app from your MyChart account at any time by deleting the app from your device, which removes the stored login token. No copy of your health information is retained anywhere else.

Children's privacy

Anchor Care: Today is designed for adult patients and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
